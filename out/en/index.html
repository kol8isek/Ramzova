Welcome to Ramzová

A place where time slows down.

For more than a century we have welcomed travellers seeking the silence of the mountains, the scent of pine and a comfort that matches the highest standards. Our accommodation sits on the slope of Šerák with a panoramic view of the Jeseníky ridges — from sunrise over Praděd to star-filled nights above the valley.
